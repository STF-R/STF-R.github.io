Origins

Building useful solutions in demanding environments.

Founded in 2020, Paradigm AI draws on experience gained both in demanding financial environments and through Data & AI projects delivered for private and public organizations.

This dual business and technical culture enables projects to be approached with particular attention to practical value, data quality and the real ability to move into production.

Assignments have included predictive modeling, data engineering, artificial intelligence solution development and, more recently, the design of agentic platforms. Paradigm AI has also worked with the public sector, where reliability, security and integration constraints are central.

Engagement contexts

Technology must work in the real world.

Paradigm AI works on challenges where data and AI must integrate into existing processes: finance, the public sector, logistics and operations, support functions, and environments requiring advanced document or analytical processing. The approach remains deliberately pragmatic: understand the business, assess feasibility, build, then secure operational use.
